About

John McKinley is a builder, investor, and board-level technology executive with over three decades of experience leading large-scale technology transformations at some of the world's most consequential organizations — Merrill Lynch, AOL, News Corp, and GE Capital — before turning to founding and backing the next generation of AI companies.

At McKinley Labs he has built three AI security products from scratch: ThreatVec, an AI Agent Security Posture Management platform for continuous red team analysis across enterprise agent portfolios; Bastion, an AI gateway that governs what reaches a model — classifying every prompt, stripping PII, hard-blocking regulated data fail-closed, and writing a hash-chained audit record of every interaction; and Snapper, an AI agent security platform that governs agents from application intent down to kernel enforcement with an eBPF runtime sentinel and self-learning sandboxing.

He has been investing in AI since 2007 — from Powerset's early natural language processing (acquired by Microsoft, integrated into Bing) through Hypergiant, DeepMoney.ai, and now Cognitum One. His portfolio across Great Falls Ventures has produced thirteen exits to acquirers including Microsoft, Google, Yahoo, Symantec, and Magic Leap. He has served on the Equifax board since 2008, currently chairs its Technology Committee, and sits on its Audit Committee.
